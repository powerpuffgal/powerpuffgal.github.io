The through-line

Most finance work comes down to one question: why did this number move, and can we show it? I started with the economics behind it, learned the discipline of the plan, then began to automate the checking.

Understand · 2022 to 2025

Economics first

At NUS I specialised in Monetary and Financial Economics. At ADSON & Bajaj Capital in India I used it on 7 client portfolio proposals, 10+ due diligence reports and 3 recommendations that clients adopted.

Challenge · 2025 to now

The discipline of the plan

At Asurion I ran the 2027 Annual Operating Plan across 6 APAC markets. Going through forecasts line by line with market leads cut one legal forecast by about 65%. When unit costs looked unfavourable, I showed the cause was a 26% volume reforecast, not a cost overrun.

Automate · 2025 to now

Make it repeatable

I led one of the region's first AI initiatives, an AI-generated SG&A commentary adopted across 6 APAC markets. I also built agentic workflows that cut a model control check from 3–5 hours to under 1.

Equity research and paper-trading agent

A Claude-driven agent that runs five market-research sessions every trading day. It screens on ROE above 15%, net margin above 10%, debt-to-equity below 1.5x and PEG below 2, and writes a thesis and an exit trigger before each trade. It holds 12 positions under hard limits: 15% maximum per position, a 10% cash floor and -15% stops. Project at github.com/powerpuffgal/trading-agent.
